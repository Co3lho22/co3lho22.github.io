About

Who I Am

I'm Diogo Coelho (aka co3lho22), a 22-year-old security enthusiast from Porto, Portugal. Currently finishing my Master's in Cybersecurity at Universidade do Porto.

I spend most of my free time diving into binary exploitation, reverse engineering malware, and tinkering with systems.

What I Do

Member of xSTF, where I compete in CTF competitions. Pwn and rev challenges are where I feel most at home.

Beyond CTFs, I'm working on applying that knowledge to real-world scenarios—reversing actual malware samples and exploring firmware extraction and exploitation. Bridging the gap between CTF skills and practical security research.

I learn best by building. I've developed projects like a simple Rust C2 to understand command and control fundamentals, and I'm currently working on a custom bootloader for ESP32 to deepen my understanding of IoT security.
